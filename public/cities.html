Why Do Gold Rates Vary Slightly Across Indian Cities?

While international spot gold (XAU/USD) and the USD/INR exchange rate set the base benchmark across India, local bullion association quotes vary slightly by city due to state-level logistics, vaulting/insurance costs, local demand volume, and regional bullion dealer spreads.
