About GoldPriceIndia.live

GoldPriceIndia.live is an independent, real-time bullion and jewellery price tracker built for Indian consumers, retail investors, and jewellers. Our goal is to provide fast, transparent, and easy-to-verify gold prices in Indian Rupees (INR) without clutter or hidden markups.

How Our Gold Rates Are Calculated

  • Global Spot Feed (XAU/USD): We fetch live international troy ounce gold prices (1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 grams) every 60 seconds.
  • Intraday USD/INR Exchange Rate: We convert international USD spot prices using live intraday USD-to-INR foreign exchange feeds with multi-provider redundancy.
  • Indian Landed Bullion Benchmark: Domestic Indian bullion prices include customs levies, AIDC, and local market parity factors to reflect realistic Indian market rates before 3% GST and making charges.
  • Karat Purity Breakdown: 22K (91.6%), 20K (83.3%), and 18K (75.0%) rates are derived proportionally from the 24K (99.9%) fine gold rate.
